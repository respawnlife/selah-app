Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions, then select Selah and cancel. You’ll keep access until the end of the current period.

Is my journal backed up?

If you turn on iCloud sync in Selah’s settings, your journal is backed up to your iCloud account. You can also export your data as CSV, JSON, or PDF from Settings (a Premium feature).

Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

Yes. On the paywall, tap “Restore Purchases” while signed into the same App Store account you used to subscribe.

How do I export my prayers?

Go to Settings → Export in the app, then choose the format you prefer.
